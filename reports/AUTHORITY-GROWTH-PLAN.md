## Current state

- Domain founded **April 2026** (`/about`).
- Public web search found **no** independent editorial citations of `toollabz.com`.
- Neon’s UK yield roundup lists competitors, not ToolLabz.
- GSC shows mid-SERP impressions on three tool URLs — eligible, not preferred.
- Competitors are aged brands (Listen To Taxman 2004, The Salary Calculator from 2006, Savills, Omni, Calculator.net).

**Verdict (unchanged):** authority is the **#1 remaining limiter for page-one** on the GSC queries, and **#2 overall** after content/intent. Numerically **unproven**.

---

## Domain-level strategy

Earn a small number of **contextual** mentions of ToolLabz as a free calculator site:

- About / methodology citations from people who already list “useful tools”
- Hub mentions (`/real-estate-tools`, `/business-tools`, `/uk-finance-tax`) only when the article is a directory of categories, not a single calculator

Do not buy homepage links. Do not run PBNs.
