## International articles

**Do not machine-translate the 102 posts.**

If a locale starts receiving GSC impressions on a catalog tool, write **one** native explainer for that tool (loan *or* margin *or* salary-generic) — not a mirrored blog tree.

Until `/fr/` is live and measured: **zero** translated articles.

---

## What not to publish

- City/country yield clones  
- “Best 50 calculators” directories  
- Autogenerated FAQs as posts  
- Legal award articles that invent case-law numbers  
- AI-detector / humanizer SEO spam  

## Author / EEAT

Keep `/about`, `/methodology`, `/editorial-policy`. Do not invent staff or fake reviews. Cite GOV.UK, HMRC, NHS, IRS, OECD only when the page actually uses those rules.
